Stop the Shop

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Stop the Shop app for iPhone ("the app") uses, why, and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain. If anything is unclear, email us at info@growix-ai.nl.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never sign up, and we never ask for your email address.
  • Most of your data stays on your iPhone. That includes your first name, if you give one.
  • We never learn which apps or websites you block. Apple's Screen Time keeps that private, even from us.
  • We use PostHog analytics to see how the app is used, so we can improve it. This is linked to a random identifier, not to your name.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no tracking across other apps or websites.

1. Who we are

Stop the Shop is provided by AIM Digital, based in the Netherlands. AIM Digital is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at info@growix-ai.nl.

2. Data that stays on your iPhone

The app stores the following on your device only. We do not upload it to our own servers, and we cannot see it.

  • Your first name, if you choose to enter one during onboarding. It is only used to greet you in the app. It stays on your device and is never sent to analytics or anywhere else.
  • Your onboarding answers: the shopping categories you pick, your triggers and habits, your estimated monthly impulse spend, your estimated daily browsing time, and your savings goal (its name and target amount).
  • Your protection history, such as when blocking was on, streaks, and Hard Lock periods.
  • Emergency pass usage: when you used a pass and how many you have left for the year.
  • Purchases you choose to log (the "I've been naughty" entries), including the amount you enter.
  • Your settings, such as reminder preferences.

Some of the answers and figures above are also sent to analytics in the form described in section 4. Your first name never is.

3. Screen Time and the apps you block

Stop the Shop uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to block shopping apps and websites. You choose what to block in Apple's own picker, not in a screen we built.

Apple only gives the app opaque tokens that stand for your choices. The app cannot read them, so we never learn which apps or websites you picked. These tokens stay on your device and are never sent to us, to analytics, or to anyone else.

4. Analytics (PostHog)

We use PostHog, a product analytics service, to understand how people use the app and to make it better. For example, it helps us see where people get stuck during onboarding, which features are useful, and whether the app actually helps people save money.

The app sends PostHog:

  • App usage events, such as opening the app, viewing screens and using features.
  • The answers and figures you enter in the app, for example your onboarding answers, your savings goal and amounts, and how you use features like emergency passes, Hard Lock and logged purchases.
  • Technical device data: device model, iOS version, app version, language, and a pseudonymous identifier that the app generates.

The app does not send your first name, and it cannot send which apps or websites you block, because it never knows them.

Because there are no accounts, this data is linked to the random identifier, not to your name or email address. It is still personal data under the GDPR, and we treat it that way. We use PostHog's EU cloud, so analytics data is stored in the European Union.

Your choice: we rely on our legitimate interest in improving the app (see section 8). You can object to this at any time by emailing info@growix-ai.nl. We may also provide a switch in the app to turn analytics off.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the app never sees your card or bank details.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. Apple and RevenueCat process your purchase and subscription status (for example, which plan you have, whether you are in a free trial, and when it renews or ends). This is linked to a pseudonymous app user ID, not to your name or email address.

Apple processes your payment under its own privacy policy, which you can read at apple.com/legal/privacy.

6. Notifications

The app only uses local notifications, which are created on your iPhone itself. These are gentle reminders, which you can switch off, and a notice when an emergency pass ends. We do not send remote push notifications or marketing messages.

7. What we never do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. That is why the app never shows Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt.

8. Why we are allowed to use your data (legal bases)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we need a legal basis for each use of personal data:

  • Analytics: our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used and improving it. We keep this data pseudonymous, limit what we collect, and you can object at any time.
  • Subscriptions: to provide the subscription you bought (performance of a contract), and to meet legal obligations such as keeping records of purchases.
  • Emails you send us: if you contact us, we use your email address and message to reply. This is based on our legitimate interest in answering your questions, or on handling your request to exercise your rights.

Data that only lives on your iPhone is not shared with us.

9. Who else processes your data

We only share data with service providers that help us run the app:

  • PostHog, for product analytics, as described in section 4.
  • RevenueCat, for subscription management, as described in section 5.
  • Apple, for the App Store, payments and the Screen Time framework.

Some of these companies may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer must be covered by safeguards that the GDPR recognises, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We may also share data if we are required to by law.

10. How long we keep data

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it or delete the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Analytics: only as long as we need it for analysis, and never longer than 24 months.
  • Purchase records: Apple and RevenueCat keep these for as long as the law requires.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your question or request.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: have incorrect data corrected.
  • Erasure: have your data deleted.
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Objection: object to our use of your data based on legitimate interest, including analytics.
  • Portability: receive your data in a common, machine-readable format.

To use any of these rights, email info@growix-ai.nl. We will reply within one month.

How to delete your data

Because there are no accounts, we cannot look you up by name or email. Here is how deletion works:

  • Data on your iPhone: delete the app. That removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Analytics data: open the app's settings and copy your identifier. Then email it to info@growix-ai.nl and ask us to delete your data. Please do this before you delete the app, because you need the app to find your identifier.
  • Subscription data: Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required by law. For data held by Apple, you can also contact Apple directly.

Complaints

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can try to fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl, or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

12. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS. Data sent to our service providers travels over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and we only collect what we need.

13. Children

Stop the Shop is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 16 has used the app and data was collected, email us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. Please check back from time to time.
