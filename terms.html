1. What the app does

Stop the Shop helps people who impulse-shop online to stop. It blocks the shopping apps and websites you choose, using Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. It also lets you:

  • set a savings goal and track your progress;
  • use emergency passes, which lift blocking for 60 minutes, from a limited yearly budget;
  • turn on Hard Lock, which means blocking cannot be switched off for 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days.

Hard Lock is designed to be hard to undo. Once you start it, you will not be able to switch blocking off in the app until the period ends, so please choose it on purpose.
